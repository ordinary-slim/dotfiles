# Commit messages

- A short, dry subject to the point, then a blank line and a bullet list of what changed; each bullet at most 2 lines.
- No long prose paragraphs.
- Never refer to points of local planning files by their labels (A, B1, B2, "item 4"…): files outside version
  control mean nothing in `git log`.
- Keep the Co-Authored-By trailer.
- A one-line subject alone is fine for trivial commits (e.g. "Version 0.14").
